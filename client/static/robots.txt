# Nothing is refused here, on purpose. Portfolios are not listed by search engines: their pages
# answer with an `x-robots-tag: noindex` header and carry a robots meta (client/src/lib/robots.ts).
# A crawler has to fetch a page to read that, and LinkedIn, Slack and the like fetch a shared
# portfolio to build its preview; a Disallow would stop both, and could not name the portfolios
# anyway (each is a top-level path, /<name>). In production Cloudflare puts its managed rules
# above these lines (docs/DEPLOYMENT.md, "Search engines").
